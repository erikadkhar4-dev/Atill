The Dining Room
Our Story

A quiet room,
a serious kitchen.

Attill sits in a wood-panelled room down the Savroli–Kharpada Road, a short walk from the Jio petrol pump and directly opposite Oberoi Mall. Inside: warm timber, a small bar glowing under neon, and separate kitchens for vegetarian and non-vegetarian orders — so the tandoor smoke and the kadai spice never cross paths.
